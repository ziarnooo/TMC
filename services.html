Seasonal care
01 — Maintenance

A wide range of home maintenance.

We care for the systems that keep your home running — proactively, on a schedule that fits the way you live. From seasonal walk-throughs to bi-monthly inspections, our team keeps small issues from becoming big ones.

Seasonal (Spring & Fall)

  • Coordinate winterizations of plumbing
  • Clean gutters & inspect roofs
  • Clean flat roofs / check drains
  • Open & close foundation vents
  • Clean icemakers
  • Coordinate window cleaning

Bi-monthly inspection items

  • Sump pumps
  • Flat roof drains
  • Plumbing fixtures
  • Water heaters
  • Generator
  • Elevator
  • Crawl space & attic
  • Light bulbs — as needed
  • Air filters — as needed
  • Exterior / pressure washing
