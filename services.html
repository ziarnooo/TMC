Maintenance
Maintenance Services

Offering a wide range of home maintenance.

Home service plans can be modified but typically include the following scheduled services.

Seasonal — Spring and Fall includes

  • Coordinate winterizations of plumbing
  • Clean gutters & inspect roofs
  • Clean flat roofs / check drains
  • Open & close foundation vents
  • Clean icemakers
  • Coordinate window cleaning

Bi-monthly maintenance includes, but not limited to inspection of

  • Sump pumps
  • Flat roof drains
  • Plumbing fixtures
  • Water heaters
  • Generator
  • Elevator
  • Crawl space and attic
  • Light bulbs – replace as needed
  • Air filters – replace as needed
  • Exterior cleaning / pressure washing

Renovations

From a single bathroom to a whole house.
