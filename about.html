About

A team of dependable home maintenance professionals.

After realizing the demand for a continual home maintenance service, we decided to establish a company that would properly tend to your residence.

Our staff remains small, as a result allowing unmatchable service and an accelerated response time to your needs as a homeowner.

There are thousands of moving parts within a home. The Maintenance Company offers clients a reliable service program which ensures that all continual maintenance needs are being managed by employees that you can trust. Inspections on various components allow a prolonged shelf life and potentially catching items at fault before they become a larger issue.

Despite the name, we are capable of handling any project that you may pursue. This includes site work, additions, and both interior/exterior renovations. Our vast skill set provides an expertise that can accomplish any job, regardless of size.

Our goal is to give you, the homeowner, an option to a hassle-free, more personable construction and home maintenance experience.
