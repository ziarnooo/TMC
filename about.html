About

An option to a hassle-free,
more personable home experience.

A team of dependable home maintenance professionals — caring for Nashville homes since 2000.

We've intentionally kept our staff small — it lets us deliver the kind of quality and responsiveness our clients deserve.

The Maintenance Company was established to meet homeowner demand for continuous, dependable maintenance services.

We manage the many systems within a home through reliable service programs and inspections that extend component lifespan and identify issues early. Beyond routine maintenance, we handle site work, additions, and full interior and exterior renovations.

Our goal is simple: to give homeowners a friendlier, more personable approach to construction and home care.
